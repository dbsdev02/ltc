# Kids Fragrance Landing Page

## Goal
Build a premium, single-product fragrance landing page that balances childlike wonder with parent-facing trust and a simple purchase path.

## What will be built
- A cinematic animated home page with clouds, stars, particles, product-focused storytelling, and reduced-motion support.
- Reusable sections for benefits, editable formulation details, fragrance experience, daily-use story, interactive product view, purchase, testimonials, parent guidance, usage, FAQ, final call-to-action, and footer.
- A sticky purchase bar after the opening section and consistent “SHOP THE MAGIC” / “BUY NOW” actions.
- Centralized editable product content, including placeholders for name, price, size, label instructions, ingredients, policies, reviews, and checkout integrations.
- Responsive layouts optimized for desktop, tablet, and mobile, with lighter effects on smaller screens.
- Route metadata, product/organization/FAQ structured data, canonical placeholder, robots configuration, and sitemap.

## Visual direction
- Soft sky blue, lavender, powder pink, cream, subtle gold, and deep navy.
- Rounded playful display typography paired with a clean modern sans-serif.
- Storybook cloud environments, premium illustrated product bottle, layered parallax, restrained glass effects, and gentle scroll transitions.

## Technical details
- Use the existing React/TanStack Start and Tailwind v4 foundation.
- Add Motion for React and Lucide icons if not already installed.
- Keep animations transform/opacity based, accessible, and responsive to `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Purchase actions remain clearly marked as demo placeholders; no live cart, inventory, payment, or shipping promises.

## Validation
- Verify the page renders and interactions work in the live preview at desktop and mobile sizes.
- Check sticky purchase behavior, smooth scrolling, FAQ keyboard controls, quantity controls, metadata, and reduced-motion behavior.
